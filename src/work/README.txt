Put your proof-of-work photos and videos in this folder
(jpg, jpeg, png, webp, avif, svg, mp4, webm, mov).
They appear in the "Proof of work" gallery automatically, sorted by file name
(work-1.jpg, work-2.jpg, ... or 01-name.jpg). Restart `npm run dev` after adding files.
Tip: keep photos under ~400 KB and videos under ~15 MB for fast loading.
The sample-*.svg files are placeholders - delete them when you add your real photos.
